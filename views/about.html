About Smart Campus Event Planner

Smart Campus Event Planner is a centralized platform designed to help university students discover and manage campus events more easily.

Our Purpose

Campus event information is often scattered across emails, posters, social media, Moodle announcements, and student group chats. Our goal is to bring this information together in one convenient place so students do not miss valuable opportunities.

For Students

Students can browse upcoming events, view event details, register or cancel registrations, and keep track of their activities through a personalized dashboard.

For Organizers

Event organizers and administrators can create and manage events, monitor registrations, track attendance, manage event capacity, and view participation statistics.

Our Goal

Our goal is to make campus life more connected, organized, and accessible by helping students find events that match their interests.
